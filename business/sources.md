## 1. Evidentiary Standards & Methodology

Every market size, growth rate, tourist arrival figure, competitor statistic, and pricing metric cited across TRAIL’s business deliverables is grounded in verifiable primary or secondary sources. 

Priority was given to:
1. **Official Government Publications:** State and national tourism departments.
2. **Institutional Market Research Reports:** Verified industry research firms (DataIntelo, Fortune Business Insights, Precedence Research).
3. **Primary Tech & Company Documentation:** Direct API pricing documentation, corporate press releases, and SEC/investor announcements.
4. **Reputable Financial & Tech Journalism:** TechCrunch, Time Magazine, Tracxn, Skift.
