## Slide 02: The Problem

- **Slide Title:** **The Blind Spot of Modern Navigation**
- **Subtitle:** *Why the fastest path is always the dullest.*
- **Visual Suggestion:** 3 metric cards highlighting the core points of friction:
  - `0% Serendipity:` Traditional GPS treats walking as dead transit friction.
  - `Screen Fixation:` Pedestrians walk with eyes glued down to a blinking blue dot.
  - `Ad-Auction Erasure:` Independent craft cafes and bookshops are buried beneath paid corporate pins.
- **Key Bullet Points:**
  - **The Transit Friction Fallacy:** Commuter apps optimize strictly for vehicular velocity, routing pedestrians along noisy highways and ignoring cobblestone alleys, stairs, and shaded paths.
  - **Cognitive Exhaustion:** Finding an authentic local spot requires 45 minutes of cross-referencing between Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Yelp, and Instagram.
  - **Merchant Vulnerability:** Independent artisans cannot afford thousands in CPC search bidding, leaving them invisible to travelers.
- **Presenter Notes (Deliverable 14):**
  > *"When you walk through a historic quarter like Fontainhas with Google Maps, the app treats every minute you spend looking at a 19th-century tile mural or stopping for an espresso as 'lost efficiency.' It constantly wants to reroute you to the nearest four-lane road. We call this the blind spot of navigation: algorithmic efficiency at the expense of human serendipity."*

## Slide 03: The Solution & Protocol

- **Slide Title:** **Cartography with a Soul: The 4-Step Protocol**
- **Subtitle:** *From Topo Sheet to Trail Step.*
- **Visual Suggestion:** 4 sequential horizontal cards matching the live platform architecture:
  `[01. DISPATCH]` -> `[02. SYNTHESIS]` -> `[03. WALK]` -> `[04. LEDGER]`
- **Key Bullet Points:**
  - **01. Dispatch:** Choose your walking radius, temporal budget (e.g., 90 minutes), and sensory mood (single-origin coffee, architecture, river breeze).
  - **02. Synthesis:** Instant generation of an offline-ready vector dossier with elevation profiles and curator clues (<4MB download).
  - **03. Walk:** Ambient, eyes-up guidance using physical "Living Landmarks" and compass bearings—keeping phones in pockets.
  - **04. Ledger:** Inscribe field notes, collect digital stamps in the Field Passport, and co-curate the community registry.
- **Presenter Notes (Deliverable 14):**
  > *"TRAIL solves this with an inverted 4-step protocol. You tell us your time and vibe; we synthesize an offline-ready dossier in under 500 milliseconds. You walk with your eyes up on the street, guided by architectural landmarks rather than turn-by-turn phone chatter. And when you reach your destination, you inscribe your discoveries into a living community ledger."*
