# 1 Trail — Product Definition
*Don't just get there. Discover along the way.*

## 01 · Product Definition
A discovery platform that helps people explore cities through curated and personalized journeys rather than isolated destinations.

### Product Logic
- **Problem**: Destination-focused discovery
- **Opportunity**: Journey-oriented discovery
- **Product**: TRAIL
- **Experience**: Connected exploration
- **Pattern**: Café → Bookstore → Street Art → Bakery → Viewpoint
- *TRAIL transforms individual places into connected exploration journeys.*

---

## 02 · What is TRAIL?
**From individual places to connected journeys.**
TRAIL connects destinations into a sequenced exploration journey based on the user's context and preferences.

### Users Choose:
1. **01 Interests**: What do you want to discover?
2. **02 Time**: How much time do you have?
3. **03 Location**: Where do you want to explore?
4. **04 Distance**: How far do you want to go?
5. **05 Preferences**: What kind of exploration suits you?

$$\downarrow$$
**TRAIL analyzes these inputs and presents a sequence of complementary places and experiences as one journey.**
*Example*: Coffee Shop → Bookstore → Street Art → Local Bakery → Sunset Point.
*TRAIL is not simply finding places. It is connecting them into a journey.*

---

## 03 · Who is TRAIL For?
### Primary Users: Curious Young Explorers
Students and young adults who enjoy exploring cities, have limited time, want less-obvious places and don't want to spend significant effort planning.

### Secondary Users: Local Explorers / Trail Creators
People who know interesting local places, enjoy creating routes, want to share experiences and can contribute curated trails.

### The Problem
People use multiple platforms to discover individual places, then manually decide:
- *What should I combine?*
- *In what order?*
- *How long will it take?*
- *Does the route make sense?*

$$\text{FRAGMENTED} + \text{PLANNING-HEAVY}$$
- **Today**: Search → Save → Compare → Manually Plan
- **TRAIL's Response**: Individual discoveries → Connected journey

---

## 04 · How Does TRAIL Work? (The Product Flow)
1. **01 Discover**: Choose what you are curious about (Food · Art · Culture · Nature · Books).
2. **02 Set your context**: Time, location and preferred distance.
3. **03 Choose interests**: Coffee, books, art, culture, nature, food.
4. **04 Get a trail**: TRAIL creates or recommends a sequence of complementary discoveries.
5. **05 Start exploring**: Follow the trail from one discovery to the next.
6. **06 Complete + share**: Finish the trail, save it or share the journey.

$$\text{INTERESTS} + \text{TIME} + \text{LOCATION} + \text{DISTANCE} \longrightarrow \text{STOP 01} \rightarrow \text{STOP 02} \rightarrow \text{STOP 03} \rightarrow \text{STOP 04}$$

---

## 05 · What Does the User Experience?
### From Curiosity to Exploration
- **01 Discover**: *"What are you curious about today?"*
- **02 Time + Distance**: *"How much time do you have?"* (1h, 2h, 3h+) | *"How far do you want to explore?"* (1km, 3km, 5km+)
- **03 Personalized Trail**: *The Slow Sunday Trail (5 Stops · 3.2 KM · 2 Hours)*
- **04 Next Discovery**: *Hidden Bookstore (12 min walk, "Worth the detour.")*
- **START TRAIL →**
*Instead of a list of places, TRAIL packages discoveries into a journey users can understand, start and experience step by step.*

---

## 06 · Why Use TRAIL? (The User Value)
1. **Less planning**: No need to research and sequence every place yourself.
2. **More discovery**: Surfaces interesting places beyond your original destination.
3. **Built around the journey**: Focuses on how places connect, not isolated destinations.
4. **Fits your context**: Interests · time · location · distance.
5. **More room for spontaneity**: Explore without planning everything beforehand.

$$\text{Before: Research} \rightarrow \text{Compare} \rightarrow \text{Save} \rightarrow \text{Plan} \rightarrow \text{Navigate}$$
$$\text{With TRAIL: Choose} \rightarrow \text{Curate} \rightarrow \text{Connect} \rightarrow \text{Explore}$$

---

## 07 · What Makes TRAIL Different?
### Destination Discovery vs. Journey Discovery
- **Traditional Discovery**: Search for a place → View individual results → Choose what interests you → Save places → Manually plan the route → Navigate.
- **TRAIL**: Choose what interests you → Set your context → Receive a connected trail → Explore discoveries in sequence → Save / share the journey.

---

## 08 · The TRAIL Value Proposition
- **FOR**: Curious young explorers
- **WHO**: Want to discover interesting places without spending significant time planning
- **IS A**: Discovery platform
- **THAT**: Creates personalized exploration journeys from connected places and experiences
- **UNLIKE**: Destination-focused discovery tools
- **TRAIL**: Organizes discoveries into a journey based on interests, time, location and distance

### Final Product Statement
> **“TRAIL helps people spend less time deciding where to go and more time discovering what’s along the way.”**
