# Assignment 05: Brand Character Pillars & Information Hierarchy

## 1. Five Brand Character Pillars
How should TRAIL feel?
TRAIL should feel like a curious local friend who knows interesting places and encourages you to take the unexpected route.

### 01 CURIOUS
- **Definition**: Seeking the story behind the scenery.
- **Keywords**: CURIOUS · OPEN · EXPLORATORY
- **Tagline**: *"We ask questions instead of making assumptions."*

### 02 UNEXPECTED
- **Definition**: Choosing the interesting path over the obvious one.
- **Keywords**: SURPRISING · DIFFERENT · SPONTANEOUS
- **Tagline**: *"Interesting way, never the fastest."*

### 03 HUMAN
- **Definition**: Rooted in people and local knowledge.
- **Keywords**: LOCAL · PERSONAL · AUTHENTIC
- **Tagline**: *"Real people. Real corners. Real stories."*

### 04 ACTIVE
- **Definition**: TRAIL represents movement, not stillness.
- **Keywords**: MOVING · ENERGETIC · DYNAMIC

### 05 CONTEMPORARY
- **Definition**: A modern lens on exploration.
- **Keywords**: MODERN · EDITORIAL · CREATIVE

## 2. Information Hierarchy Principle
$$\text{SEE} \longrightarrow \text{UNDERSTAND} \longrightarrow \text{EXPLORE} \longrightarrow \text{ACT}$$

1. **01 / FIRST — DISCOVER**:
   - **Trail Name**: The main destination or trail name gets the strongest visual emphasis (`THE HIDDEN COFFEE TRAIL`).
2. **02 / ORIENT — KNOW**:
   - **Time · Distance · Stops**: Quick telemetry scan helps users understand the journey at a glance (`5 STOPS · 3.2 KM · 2 HOURS`).
3. **03 / EXPLORE — UNDERSTAND**:
   - **Description**: A short, atmospheric description explains what makes the trail worth discovering (`Local cafés, independent bookstores, hidden streets and a sunset worth staying for.`).
4. **04 / ACT — START**:
   - **Start Trail →**: The final action is clear but visually secondary to discovery (`START TRAIL →`).
