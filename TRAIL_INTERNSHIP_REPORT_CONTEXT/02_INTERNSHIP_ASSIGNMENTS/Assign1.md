# Assignment 01: IT Services Ecosystem & Organizational Architecture

## 1. Team Structure
1. **Top management**: Senior leadership take the major business and strategy decisions.
2. **Business**: Teams are often divided by industries such as banking, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, telecom, energy and government.
3. **Service & technology units**: These can include cloud, application development, data, cybersecurity, engineering, consulting, infrastructure, testing and business process services.
4. **Project & delivery teams**: Project managers, technical leads, developers, testers, business analysts, designers, DevOps engineers and other specialists work together on the actual project.
5. **Support functions**: HR, finance, recruitment, legal, sales, learning and development, procurement and internal IT support the main business.
6. **Delivery centres**: A lot of work is distributed across different cities and countries. This makes 24/7 delivery possible for global clients.

### Examples:
- **Infosys** describes go-to-market business units by industries such as Financial Services and Insurance, Communications, Energy/Utilities, Manufacturing, Hi-Tech and Life Sciences/Healthcare.
- **Wipro** describes strategic market units and global business lines.
- **HCLTech** publicly separates its businesses into IT and Business Services, Engineering and R&D Services, HCLSoftware, and AI/GenAI.

## 3. Common Strengths
1. **Large talent pool**: The biggest companies can provide many developers, testers, consultants and specialists. This helps when a client needs a large team quickly.
2. **Global delivery**: Many of these firms have offices and delivery centres in different countries, so projects can continue across time zones.
3. **Industry knowledge**: Because they work with many clients, teams often develop specialised knowledge in banking, healthcare, telecom, retail, manufacturing and other industries.
4. **Process and documentation**: Large service companies normally have established project management, security, quality and delivery processes.
5. **Wide technology coverage**: One company can provide cloud, software development, testing, data, cybersecurity, consulting and infrastructure services instead of the client hiring many vendors.

---

## 4. Common Weaknesses
1. **Large hierarchy**: Because there are many management levels, decisions can sometimes take longer than in a small company.
2. **Project dependency**: An employee's experience can depend heavily on which client or project they are assigned to. Two people in the same company can have very different work experiences.
3. **Less control for fresh employees**: New employees may have limited choice over technology, project or location at the beginning of their career.
4. **Routine work risk**: Service projects can sometimes involve maintenance, support, testing or repetitive work. This may not give every employee the same level of development exposure.
5. **Client dependency**: Revenue and work are linked to client budgets and global economic conditions. If clients reduce technology spending, hiring and project demand can be affected.
