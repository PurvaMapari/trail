## 3. Brand Voice: Words That Wander With Purpose
*TRAIL doesn't give directions — it offers invitations. We write like a curious local pulling you toward the next doorway, the next detour, the small thing worth noticing on the way to somewhere else.*

### Five Voice Principles
1. **Question-First**: We open with wonder, not instructions. Every line assumes the reader wants to be pulled somewhere. (*"What if the long way is the good way?"*)
2. **Off-Route**: We phrase things sideways. Where others say faster or direct, we say stranger, quieter, further in. (*"Skip the main road. There's a bakery three turns from here."*)
3. **First-Person Local**: We write like someone who lives here. Specific streets, real hours, honest opinions. Never generic, never algorithmic. (*"My favourite bench in the city is on this trail."*)
4. **In Motion**: Verbs lead. We favour walking, wandering, turning, following. Copy should feel like it's already moving forward. (*"Keep walking. The trail bends left after the mural."*)
5. **Edited, Not Decorated**: Modern, spare, editorial. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype. One good sentence beats three eager ones. (*"Six stops. About an hour. Worth every step."*)

### Editorial Dos and Don'ts
- **DO**: Ask, don't announce; Name the specific thing; Suggest, never insist; Move the reader forward; Sound like a person.
- **DON'T**: Say "best" or "top"; Promise efficiency; Shout with punctuation; Explain the obvious; Sound like a database.
